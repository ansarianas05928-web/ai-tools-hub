About Us

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Free AI Tools Hub is a free website with two parts: a set of small utilities that run entirely in your browser, and a hand-picked directory of AI software for writing, design, coding, audio/video and job applications.

What we offer

  • Free browser tools: Prompt Builder, Text Summarizer (English and Hindi), Word Counter, JSON Formatter and Password Generator. They run locally in JavaScript, so the text you enter is not sent to our servers.
  • AI tools directory: 30+ products with a short description, a category and a link to the official site, so you can compare options without searching ten different pages.
  • Guides: plain-language explanations of how to get better results from each tool.

How we choose tools

We list real, publicly available products that offer a free tier or a free trial at the time of writing. Features, limits and prices of third-party products change often, so please confirm the details on the tool's own website before you sign up or pay.

How we earn money

The site is free to use. We may show advertising (such as Google AdSense), and some outbound links may be affiliate links, which means we can earn a commission if you buy a paid plan through them, at no extra cost to you. Affiliate links are marked as sponsored in the page code, and a commission does not change what we write about a tool. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Who we are

Free AI Tools Hub is an independent project. Questions, corrections or tool suggestions are welcome through the Contact page.
